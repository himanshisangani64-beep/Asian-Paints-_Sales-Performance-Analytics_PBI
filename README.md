## 📌 Project Overview

The **Asian Paints – Sales & Performance Analytics Dashboard** (Data Period: 01 January 2022 – 31 December 2025) is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI.

The project transforms raw sales, product, regional, and dealer data into meaningful business insights to help understand revenue growth, profitability, regional performance, inventory turnover, dealer efficiency, and future sales trends.

### 🎯 Project Focus Areas

| 📊 Executive Overview | 🗺️ Regional Analysis | 📦 Product Analysis | 🤝 Dealer Performance | 📈 Trends & Forecasting |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Revenue & Profit | Zone & State Analysis | Product Profitability | Dealer Efficiency | YoY Growth |
| KPI Tracking | Regional Contribution | Category Performance | Dealer Tier Analysis | Revenue Forecast |

## 🎯 Business Problem & Objectives

### 🧩 Business Problem

Managing nationwide paint sales across **500 products, 7 product categories, 5,000 dealers, and 5 major Indian regions** creates challenges in monitoring sales targets, identifying profitable regions, analyzing product performance, managing inventory, and evaluating dealer contributions.

The objective is to develop an interactive Power BI dashboard that provides a complete overview of sales and business performance.

### 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze total revenue, profit, profit margin, and order performance.
- Compare current-year and previous-year business performance.
- Identify top-performing regions, states, and cities.
- Evaluate product category and SKU-level profitability.
- Analyze inventory turnover across product categories.
- Measure dealer performance and dealer tier contributions.
- Track monthly and quarterly revenue trends.
- Generate a 6-month revenue forecast for future planning.

### 📝 Key Assumptions

- Reporting period: January 2022 – December 2025.
- Reporting currency: Indian Rupees (₹).
- Regional analysis covers five major Indian regions.
- Product analysis covers 500 SKUs and 7 categories.
- Dealer analysis covers 5,000 dealers.
- YoY comparisons use previous-year performance.
- Forecasting is based on historical revenue trends.
